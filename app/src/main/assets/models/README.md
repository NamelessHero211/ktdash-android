# Miniature reference images

These small reference images are extracted from the official Kill Team team-selection illustrations published by Games Workshop at https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/downloads/kill-team/ . They are matched by team and operative type, and are representative models rather than a guarantee of a particular weapon build. Uploaded roster photos take priority.

Miniatures and photographs are copyright Games Workshop Limited. This app is unofficial and is not endorsed by Games Workshop. `catalog.json` records each source PDF URL, page, caption and operative type. Images are arranged in 256-pixel tiles in the team WebP atlases.
